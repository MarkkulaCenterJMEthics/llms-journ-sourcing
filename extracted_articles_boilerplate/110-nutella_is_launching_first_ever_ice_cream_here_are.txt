Credit : Wells Enterprises NEED TO KNOW Nutella is launching ice cream in 14-ounce containers and single-serve wafer cones This marks Nutella’s first frozen product and follows its recent release of Nutella Peanut, its first new flavor everA Nutella jar recently went viral after being spotted aboard the Artemis II Orion spacecraft during a livestream Nutella is rolling out its popular hazelnut-cocoa spread in ice cream for the first time. The brand announced on Wednesday, May 13, that it’s launching two new frozen products that feature Nutella layers and swirls. Nutella Ice Cream, which comes in 14-ounce containers, offers “a rich, multi-textured, spoonable format,” per a release shared with PEOPLE. It can be found in stores for a suggested price starting at $4.36. The new ice cream is also available in 3.04-ounce single-serve wafer cones, made for “spontaneous moments on the go.” A four pack has a suggested retail price starting at $5.47. “This is the freezer-aisle debut that's been a long time coming,” the brand adds. Nutella ice cream. Wells Enterprises Food Network star Alex Guarnaschelli commented on the brand's Instagram announcement with a series of clapping hands emojis. Last month, Nutella announced that it was releasing a new flavor for the first time in its over 60 year history, Nutella Peanut. The new flavor is now available nationwide in major retailers. “This marks the brand's first flavor innovation in its over 60 year history, delivering a new taste experience that blends the unmistakable creaminess of Nutella with the delicious taste of roasted peanuts,” the brand, which began in 1964, said in an April 15 press release. While Nutella has previously launched snacks such as Nutella Biscuits and Nutella B-ready, Nutella Peanut marks its first new flavor. Nutella cone. The release added that the new flavor is “perfectly spreadable” for a sandwich or other treats at “afternoon snack time.” Nutella recently went viral when a jar of the spread was captured flying through the Artemis II Orion spacecraft during the mission’s livestream on April 6. “It happened just minutes before the Artemis II crew traveled the longest distance humans have ever gone into space,” the narrator of a clip CNN posted of the moment commented. “Best Nutella commercial ever 😂,” one commenter wrote on the video.
